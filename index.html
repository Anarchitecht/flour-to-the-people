What We Grow

We are a small, appointment-based plant nursery in Gatesville focused on Texas native plants and ecologically friendly, non-invasive plants suited to the Cross Timbers and Blackland Prairie regions.

Texas nativesNon-invasiveRegionally adaptedPollinator friendly

Small On Purpose

Family run. Small scale. Appointment based. We try to keep waste low and use as little disposable plastic as practical. The point is useful plants, sane quantities, and fewer pointless inputs.

Come looking for plants that make sense where you actually live, not generic landscaping shipped halfway across the continent.

Why Plant This Stuff?

💧Use Less WaterRegionally adapted plants can reduce irrigation demand once established.
🐛Feed BugsNative insects need native host plants. More insects means more functional food webs.
🦋Build BiodiversityPlants can provide nectar, seed, shelter, host material, and seasonal habitat.
🌡️Handle Texas WeatherGood regional choices are better positioned for heat, drought, and weather extremes.
🏡Improve the PropertyHealthy, distinctive landscapes can improve curb appeal and landscape usefulness.
🌎Do More Than DecorateA yard can function as habitat, soil cover, cooling vegetation, and living infrastructure.

Our Region

We focus on plants appropriate for central and north-central Texas ecological conditions, especially the Cross Timbers and Blackland Prairie. That means paying attention to heat, rainfall variability, soils, wildlife value, and whether a plant behaves itself.

No invasive-plant nonsense.

How To Visit

By appointment. This is not a big-box garden center with automatic doors and twelve aisles of fluorescent fertilizer. Contact us before coming out so someone can actually be there.
